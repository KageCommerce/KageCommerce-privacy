KageCommerce Integration Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 19, 2026

KageCommerce Integration provides tools that help authorized users connect to and manage information associated with their eBay accounts. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, protected, and deleted.

Information We Collect

When you connect your eBay account, the application may access information permitted by the eBay permissions you approve, including account identifiers, listings, inventory, orders, offers, returns, inquiries, and related selling information.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to provide requested application features, connect with eBay, display selling information, manage authorized eBay activity, troubleshoot problems, and maintain application security.

Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared only with service providers needed to operate the application, when required by law, or when necessary to protect users and the application.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. Authentication credentials and tokens are stored securely and are not intentionally exposed in public application code.

Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application’s services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and maintain security.

Users may disconnect their eBay account or request deletion of their information by contacting us. Marketplace account deletion notifications received from eBay will be processed in accordance with applicable requirements.

Your Choices

You may choose not to connect your eBay account. You may also revoke the application's access through your eBay account settings.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the application, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated effective date will appear at the top of this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, email: kagecommercehelp@gmail.com
